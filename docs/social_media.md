## 💼 LinkedIn Post

**Headline:** Transforming Digital Identity: Anonymous Passport Verification on Starknet 🌍

**Post:**
Digital identity today is broken. We’re forced to choose between leaking sensitive data to centralized honeypots or remaining excluded from secure digital services.

At **Noah**, we’re pioneering a third path. 🛡️

We’ve built a privacy-first identity layer that allows users to verify their physical e-Passport without ever revealing personal data to a third party or the blockchain.

**How it works:**
1️⃣ **Secure Upload**: User uploads a passport photo within their browser.
2️⃣ **Client-Side OCR**: MRZ data is extracted locally using Tesseract.js. No sensitive data ever leaves the device.
3️⃣ **Privacy-Preserving Proof**: A ZK-SNARK (Zero-Knowledge Proof) is generated via our SDK.
4️⃣ **On-Chain Verification**: The proof is verified on #Starknet using highly optimized #Garaga verifiers, providing an immutable record of verification without compromising user privacy.

This architecture solves the tension between compliance and privacy for Gaming, DeFi, and Consumer apps.
